THE AUSTRALIAN BONITO

🐟

Sarda australis


Eastern Coast Pelagic Hunter Hard Fighter
🌊
Native Australian Species
The Australian Bonito (Sarda australis) is found along the eastern and south-eastern coastline — from Queensland down through NSW to Victoria. A true blue Aussie fish!
⚡
Speed & Power
Built for speed with a streamlined, torpedo-shaped body. Bonito are aggressive surface feeders that strike fast and fight hard — perfect for light tackle action.
📏
Size & Weight
Typically grow to 50–75 cm and 2–5 kg, with some bruisers reaching over 8 kg. School fish often run together in size — find one, find hundreds!
🍽️
On The Table
Rich, dark red flesh with intense flavour — phenomenal smoked, sashimi-style, or grilled with lemon. Best eaten fresh from the ocean. Bonito is seriously underrated on Australian tables.

HOT SPOTS 🎯

⚓
Jervis Bay
NSW — South Coast
Crystal clear water, spectacular geography and schools of Bonito patrolling from October through April. Best from the headlands and boat in 20–40m.
🛥️ Boat 🎣 Shore Oct–Apr
🌅
Montague Island
NSW — Narooma
World-class pelagic fishing. The nutrient-rich waters bring massive Bonito schools alongside tuna and kingfish. A bucket-list Aussie location.
🛥️ Boat Only Offshore Sep–May
🏖️
Noosa Heads
QLD — Sunshine Coast
Noosa's points and bays attract Bonito from August. The national park headlands offer epic land-based action when schools push baitfish to the surface.
🎣 Shore Light Tackle Aug–Jan
🦅
Port Stephens
NSW — Hunter Region
Famous for its dolphins, Port Stephens also delivers cracking Bonito fishing around Broughton Island and the offshore reefs. Deep-water access is easy here.
🛥️ Boat Offshore Year Round
🌊
Eden & Twofold Bay
NSW — Far South Coast
The orca-famous Eden is also a Bonito haven. Deep canyon water just offshore means pelagics are always close. Run-ins with Bonito schools mid-winter are legendary here.
🛥️ Boat Canyon May–Sep
🐚
Mornington Peninsula
VIC — Port Phillip Bay
Victoria's best-kept Bonito secret. Schools chase whitebait through the bay in summer. Light spinning gear off the pier or kayak in the bay — exhilarating fun.
🛶 Kayak 🎣 Pier Nov–Mar

PRO TIPS 💡

01 Match the Hatch — Lure Selection
▶
Bonito are sight feeders so matching bait size is critical. Small silver metal slices (15–30g), micro jigs, and hard-body lures in the 60–90mm range work brilliantly. When whitebait is the target prey, go small and chrome. Pilchard-imitating lures in 80–100mm clean up when larger bait is around.
02 Find the Birds, Find the Bonito
▶
Look for bird activity — gannets diving, shearwaters wheeling — that's a guaranteed sign of baitfish being pushed up from below. Bonito (and tuna) are usually right underneath. Move quickly; schools are fast and won't stay in one spot long. Position upwind/upcurrent of the action.
03 Light Tackle = Maximum Fun
▶
Bonito are speed demons — a 3 kg fish on 8 lb braid and a 2000-series reel is an absolute riot. Go light and you'll appreciate what a powerful fish the Bonito truly is. A 7–8 ft light spin rod with a fast tip handles the aggressive strikes perfectly. Use 10–15 lb fluorocarbon leader.
04 Early Morning & Late Arvo Sessions
▶
Bonito feed most actively at low-light periods. The first two hours after sunrise and the last hour before sunset are absolutely prime. They push baitfish to the surface, creating surface explosions that are one of fishing's greatest sights. Get on the water early!
05 Bleeding & Icing for Table Quality
▶
Bonito flesh deteriorates fast if not handled properly. Bleed the fish immediately by cutting the gills and placing it head-down in a bucket of saltwater for 3–5 minutes. Then ice immediately — a 1:1 slurry of ice and saltwater is ideal. Good handling transforms Bonito from 'okay' to absolutely outstanding table fare.
06 Trolling Spreads for Covering Ground
▶
When searching for schools, troll a spread of 4–6 lures at 6–8 knots. Run bibbed minnows and pushers in a staggered spread behind the boat. Vary depths with planers or different lure designs. Once you hook up, mark the GPS position and work the area with cast & retrieve for multiple fish.
